Changes: the only edits are mechanical, so NSH can draw and tint the files:
explicit width/height 24 in place of 1em, a solid #000000 fill in place of
currentColor (the app tints the template with its theme color), and the
inline style attribute removed. Paths are unchanged.

Use: Metro shows a mark only next to the provider's own name, to identify
that provider in Settings > Model providers. The marks are the trademarks of
their respective owners. Showing them implies no endorsement of or by Metro.
Providers without a mark here (custom, self-hosted and private endpoints,
and any vendor not listed) get a lettermark drawn from their own name.

The files are bundled into the app. Nothing is fetched at runtime.
